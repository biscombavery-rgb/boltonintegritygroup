What We Do

Helping Timeshare Owners Navigate a Path Forward.

At Bolton Integrity Group, we assist timeshare owners in navigating the process of resolving unwanted ownership obligations through a structured and professional approach.

Every ownership situation is different. Some clients are dealing with rising maintenance fees, while others may be facing loan balances, financial hardship, inherited ownerships, or contracts they no longer use or benefit from. Our role is to help evaluate each situation individually and identify potential paths forward based on the owner's specific circumstances.

Discuss Your Situation
Rising Maintenance Fees

Guidance for owners burdened by maintenance costs that continue to climb each year.

Loan Balances & Hardship

Support for clients dealing with outstanding loan balances or financial hardship tied to their ownership.

Inherited Ownerships

Help for individuals who have unexpectedly inherited a timeshare and are unsure of next steps.

Unused Contracts

Assistance for owners with contracts they no longer use, need, or benefit from.

Our Process

A Clear, Structured Path Forward.

Ownership Review

We begin by reviewing the details of the ownership, including the resort, financial obligations, usage history, and any relevant documentation. This allows us to better understand the available options and potential challenges involved.

Case Evaluation

After reviewing the ownership, we help clients understand possible resolution paths that may be available depending on their situation. Because every resort and ownership structure is different, no two cases are handled exactly the same way.

Guided Support & Communication

Navigating timeshare-related processes can often be time-consuming and difficult to manage alone. We assist clients throughout the process by helping organize communication, documentation, and next steps while keeping them informed along the way.

Ongoing Client Support

We believe clear communication is an important part of professional service. Our team works to provide consistent updates and realistic expectations throughout each stage of the process.
